---
name: CatalogKit notification scope
description: The agreed boundary for team email alerts when activity happens in CatalogKit.
---

Send team email alerts for important creations, including new accounts, catalogs, payment requests, setup requests, and contact/support messages. Do not treat every upload or profile edit as an alert-worthy event.

**Why:** The user selected important creations instead of every activity when asked to clarify the desired notification volume; routine actions would create unnecessary inbox noise.

**How to apply:** When adding a new creation flow, consider a team alert after successful persistence. Do not automatically email the team for ordinary edits, uploads, or background activity.
